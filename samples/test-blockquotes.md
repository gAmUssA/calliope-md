# Blockquote rendering

A single-line quote:

> This tiny thing changed the way I ride.

A quote with two paragraphs, separated by a blank quoted line:

> Look, Garmin makes seriously good hardware.
>
> And most of us are already carrying a device with a better screen.

A quote wrapping several source lines:

> So I've been riding an app called Cadence.
> It pairs with my power pedals.
> It pairs with my heart rate strap.

A quote holding other markup:

> **Bold**, *italic*, `code`, and a [link](https://example.com).
>
> - a list item
> - another one

Nested:

> Outer quote.
>
> > Inner quote.

Right after a paragraph, no blank line:
> Lazy-adjacent quote.

End.
